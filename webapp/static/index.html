Opening range

The range is the high and low of the range timeframe from the open. A signal is the first entry-timeframe close outside it; entry is the next bar's open.

Entry & filters Default

A stop order fills on the first touch of the range edge; its fill minute can only stop out. Leave a range bound blank to switch it off. 1344 bars of 15 minutes is 14 days.

Trade rules

The selected stop method controls which multiple is used.

Trailing stop Off

With a trail set there is no fixed target: from the start level the stop follows each entry-bar close by the ATR multiple and only tightens. Leave it blank for a fixed target.

Costs & account —

Costs are modelled assumptions. The run uses M1 bars and their recorded spread.

One run at a time. Data stays on this computer. No MT5 orders are placed.
